One ink for the whole kitchen. A solid line is a fact. A dashed line is a suggestion from AI.

  • No colour carries meaning

    Weight, fill, and position do the work, so a screen reads the same in sunlight, in the dark theme, and for colour-blind cooks. The only colour on screen is the food in photographs.

  • Thumb first

    The main action sits in the lower half of the screen, and nothing you tap is smaller than 48 points.

  • Numbers line up

    Prices, weights, and days use tabular figures and sit on the right edge, like a receipt.

How the list is made

  1. Take the plan. Every ingredient of the dishes you picked, scaled to your portions.
  2. Subtract what you have. Fridge, pantry, and staples drop out.
  3. Merge and round. The same ingredient from two dishes becomes one line, rounded up to a pack.
  4. Add the extras. Base items for a cuisine, and anything someone adds by hand.
  5. Plan the trip. Lines are priced at each shop nearby, and the cheapest plan with the fewest shops wins.

Only the swap is dashed, because only that comes from AI. An item ticked in the shop moves into the fridge.
